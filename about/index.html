About Anand Pandey

I’m an SEO professional and owner of a Leading SEO agency “Artha Social” who believes that every business – whether it’s a clinic in Ranchi, a real estate firm in Pune, or a school in a Tier 3 town – deserves to be found on Google.

I started my SEO journey working with local businesses in Jharkhand. Within a year, I had helped businesses across industries – real estate, healthcare, restaurants, and schools – improve their Google rankings, attract more relevant traffic, and convert that traffic into real enquiries and customers.

What I realised early on is that the problem is almost always the same, regardless of the city or industry: great businesses are invisible online. Not because they aren’t good enough. Simply because nobody has helped them show up where their customers are already searching.

That’s what I fix.

Today I work with businesses across India – from metros to Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities – helping them build a strong, lasting presence on Google.

When a client calls me to say “I got five new enquiries from Google this week” – that’s what makes all the work worth it. I’m not just doing SEO. I’m helping someone’s business actually grow.
