How I work

Every site arrives with a different problem, so I do not start with a fixed checklist. I work out where the gap is, then pick the work that closes it.

  1. Understand the market first

    Keyword research and clustering, intent mapping, and competitor analysis before anything gets written or changed. Skipping this is how sites end up perfectly optimised for terms nobody searches.

  2. Build what does not exist yet

    Where there is demand and no page to meet it, I plan and brief new content. Target keyword, intent, heading structure, subtopics, and internal links, so a writer never has to guess what the page is for.

  3. Repair what already exists

    Most sites are sitting on wins they have already paid for. I find pages stuck in positions 8 to 20, read the SERP to see what they are missing, and rebuild rather than tweak.

  4. Clear the technical blockers

    Good content on a badly built site does not rank. Crawl and indexation issues, site and URL structure, schema, Core Web Vitals, and orphaned pages. I implement most fixes myself rather than handing over a list.

  5. Local search, where it applies

    For businesses that serve a place rather than the internet, the map pack matters more than the blue links. Listing completeness, categories and services, review and photo signals, and the landing page each profile points to.

The result at NoBroker: 10 to 12 briefs a week, and refurbished pages typically moving from page 2 or 3 onto page 1 with impressions up around 20 to 25 percent.
