Frequently Asked Questions

Can I choose the specific keywords for my business?

Yes. Keyword selection is a collaborative process. I research high-impact search terms based on your industry, location, and competition, and we finalize the ones that align with your business goals.

What do I need to provide when we start working together?

You’ll only need to share basic business details, website access (if available), and your goals. I handle the research, analysis, and optimization process from there.

Can SEO deliver instant results?

No – and anyone promising instant rankings isn’t using sustainable methods. SEO is a long-term strategy that builds credibility and visibility gradually for lasting growth.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Typically, noticeable improvements begin within 8-12 weeks, depending on your website’s current condition, competition level, and the scope of optimization.

How will I know SEO is actually working?

You receive a clear monthly report covering keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, Google Search Console data, and the specific actions taken. You’ll always know exactly what changed and why – no mystery, no confusion.
