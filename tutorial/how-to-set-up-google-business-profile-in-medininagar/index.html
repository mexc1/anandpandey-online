What Is Google Business Profile and Why It Matters for Medininagar Businesses

Before we get into the setup, it is important that you understand what Google Business Profile actually is and why it should be the very first thing you do for your online presence.

How GBP Gets You Into Google’s Map Pack - Where Real Clicks Happen

When someone in Medininagar searches for something like “best hardware shop near me” or “SEO services in Medininagar,” the first thing that appears on Google is not a regular website. It is a block of three local businesses shown on a map. This is called the Google Map Pack, and it sits right at the top of search results – above everything else.

The businesses that appear in this Map Pack get the overwhelming majority of clicks and phone calls. Research consistently shows that the top 3 results on Google Maps receive far more attention than even the highest-ranking websites below them. If you are not in the Map Pack, you are essentially invisible to local customers who are ready to buy.

Your Google Business Profile is the only way to get into this Map Pack. Without a GBP, you simply cannot appear there, no matter how good your website is.

Pro Tip: Think of your GBP as your digital storefront on Google. Just like a physical shop needs a signboard so people can find it, your business needs a GBP so Google can show it to local customers.

This is especially important in a growing city like Medininagar, where more and more residents are becoming digitally active and using their smartphones to find local services every day.

What You Must Prepare Before Creating Your Profile

Rushing into the setup without preparation is one of the most common mistakes local business owners make. Taking 10 minutes to get these details right before you start will save you from confusion and errors during the process.

Choosing Your Business Name the Right Way for Local SEO Advantage

The name you enter on your Google Business Profile must be your real, legal business name – exactly as it appears on your shop signboard, visiting card, and other platforms. This is not the place to stuff keywords or add your city name if it is not part of your official business name.

For example, if your business is registered as “Raj Electronics,” your GBP name should be “Raj Electronics” – not “Best Electronics Shop in Medininagar” or “Raj Electronics Medininagar.” Google strictly penalises keyword stuffing in business names and can suspend your profile for it.

That said, if your business name naturally includes your core service and city, you are at a genuine ranking advantage. Businesses whose names match what customers are searching for tend to rank higher in local results. If you are just starting out and have not registered yet, keep this in mind when choosing your business name.

Pro Tip: Consistency is everything. The name on your GBP must match exactly with what appears on your website, social media pages, and any other online directory where your business is listed.

Picking the Most Accurate Business Category for Medininagar Searches

Your primary business category is one of the most powerful ranking signals in your entire GBP. Google uses this to decide which searches your profile should appear for. Choosing the wrong category – or a vague one – can significantly hurt your visibility in local Medininagar searches.

Choose the category that most precisely describes what your business does, not the broadest category available. For example, if you run a clinic, do not just select “Health” – look for “General Practitioner” or the specific type of clinic you operate. If you run an SEO agency, choose “Internet Marketing Service” rather than just “Marketing Agency.”

Google also allows you to add secondary categories. Use these to cover additional services your business offers, but keep your primary category tightly focused on your main service.

NAP Details - Why Your Name, Address and Phone Must Be Exactly Consistent

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone Number. These three pieces of information are the foundation of your local SEO, and they must be identical everywhere your business appears online – your GBP, your website, JustDial, IndiaMART, Facebook, and any other platform.

Even small inconsistencies – like writing “Medininagar” in one place and “Medinīnagar” in another, or using a different phone number on your website – confuse Google and can hurt your local rankings.

Before you begin the GBP setup, decide on the exact version of your business name, full address, and primary phone number that you will use consistently across every platform. Write it down and keep it as your reference.

Have this ready before you start: Full legal business name, Complete address with PIN code, Primary local phone number, Business email address, Website URL (if you have one), Business hours for each day of the week

Step-by-Step - How to Create Your Google Business Profile

Now that you have everything ready, it is time to create your profile. Follow each step in order – do not skip ahead, as each step builds on the previous one.

Step 1 - Setting Up or Signing Into Your Google Account

Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile

Open your browser and go to business.google.com. You will be prompted to sign in with a Google account.

Step 2: Use a dedicated business Google account

It is strongly recommended that you use a separate Google account created specifically for your business – not your personal Gmail. This keeps your business activities separate and also gives you easier access to other Google tools like Google Analytics and Google Ads in the future.

If you do not have a business Google account yet, click “Create account” and set one up using your business name or a professional email format. Once signed in, click “Manage Now” to begin the setup process.

Pro Tip: Never use a personal Gmail account for your business GBP. If the account gets compromised or you need to give access to someone else later, it becomes complicated. A clean, business-only Google account makes everything easier.

Step 2 - Entering Your Business Name, Category and Address in Medininagar

Step 1: Enter your business name

Type your exact business name as you prepared it earlier. Google will show you suggestions as you type – if your business already exists in their database, you may see it listed. If it appears, you can claim it. If not, select “Create a business with this name” to proceed.

Step 2: Select your primary business category

A search box will appear asking you to choose a category. Type the most relevant term for your business and select from the dropdown. Remember – be specific, not broad. This category tells Google which searches to show your profile for, so choose carefully.

Step 3: Add your business address

If you have a physical location in Medininagar that customers can visit, enter your full address here. Include your street, locality, city (Medininagar), state (Jharkhand), and PIN code. After entering the address, Google will ask you to place a pin on the map – make sure the pin is dropped at your exact location, not just somewhere nearby.

Double-check the pin placement carefully. If the pin is in the wrong location, customers trying to find you using Google Maps will be directed to the wrong place, which leads to lost business and frustration.

Step 3 - Choosing Between a Storefront Location vs Service Area Setup

After entering your address, Google will ask you whether you also serve customers at their location. Your answer here determines how your profile is set up and how it appears on Google Maps.

If you are a storefront business: You have a physical shop or office that customers come to – like a retail store, clinic, restaurant, or salon. Select “No” when asked if you also serve customers outside your location. Your address will be shown publicly on your profile and on Google Maps.

If you are a service area business: You go to your customers rather than them coming to you – like a plumber, electrician, tutor, or delivery service. Select “Yes” and then define the areas you serve. You can hide your physical address in this case, and instead list Medininagar and surrounding areas as your service zones.

If you are both: Some businesses have a physical location and also visit customers. In this case, select “Yes” and add your service areas in addition to keeping your address visible.

Important for Medininagar service businesses: Google has made it clear that having a physical location GBP – even for service area businesses – tends to rank better in competitive local searches. If you have an office or any fixed address you can use, it is worth listing it.

How to Verify Your Google Business Profile

Verification is the step that makes your profile go live and visible on Google. Until your profile is verified, it will not appear in search results or on Google Maps. This step is non-negotiable – you must complete it.

Verification Methods Google Offers and Which One to Choose

Google offers several verification methods depending on your business type and location. Here are the options you may be presented with:

  • Postcard by mail: Google sends a physical postcard to your business address in Medininagar with a 5-digit verification code. This is the most common method for new businesses and typically takes 5 to 14 days to arrive.
  • Phone call or SMS: Google calls or texts your registered phone number with a verification code. This is faster and is available for some business types.
  • Email: A verification code is sent to your business email. This option is available for certain business categories.
  • Video verification: Google may ask you to record a short video showing your business location, signage, and operations. This has become more common in 2025-26 as Google works to reduce fake listings.
  • Instant verification: If your business is already verified on Google Search Console, you may get instant verification. This is less common for new businesses.

For most Medininagar businesses, postcard verification will be the primary option. Do not be discouraged by the wait – it is worth it.

Step-by-Step: Completing Your Postcard or Video Verification

For postcard verification:

  1.     After submitting your profile, Google will show you a confirmation screen saying the postcard is on its way.
  2.     Do not make any major changes to your address during this period, as it may reset the verification process.
  3.     Watch for a white postcard from Google in your mailbox. It will be addressed to your business at your Medininagar address.
  4.     Once received, log back into business.google.com, click “Verify” and enter the 5-digit code from the postcard.
  5.     Your profile will then go live on Google.

For video verification:

  1. Google will send you a link or prompt you to record a video within the GBP dashboard.
  2. The video should show your business signage, the surrounding area (street, building), and proof of operation (you working, products, equipment).
  3. Record in one continuous shot without cuts. Google’s reviewers watch for authenticity.
  4. Once submitted, Google typically reviews video verifications within a few days.

Pro Tip: For video verification, film during daylight hours and make sure your business signboard is clearly visible in the video. Speak naturally if you choose to narrate – reviewers appreciate authenticity over perfection.

What to Do If Verification Fails or Gets Delayed

Verification issues are more common than you might think. Here is what to do in each situation:

  • Postcard never arrived: If your postcard has not arrived after 14 days, log into your GBP dashboard and request a new one. Make sure your address is entered correctly before requesting again.
  • Code expired: Postcard codes expire after 30 days. If yours has expired, simply request a new postcard from your dashboard.
  • Video verification rejected: If Google rejects your video, they will usually tell you why. Common reasons include unclear signage, the location not matching your address, or a video that appears edited. Re-record following their feedback and resubmit.
  • Profile suspended during verification: This can happen if Google flags something inconsistent in your profile. Do not panic. Log into your GBP dashboard and look for a “Request Review” option. Ensure all your information is accurate before submitting the review request.

Never create a second GBP if your first one is pending verification or has been suspended. Multiple listings for the same business violate Google’s policies and can result in both profiles being permanently removed.

What to Do Immediately After Your Profile Gets Verified

The moment your profile is verified, it goes live on Google. But your work is not quite done yet. There are a few important checks to complete right away to make sure everything is accurate and your profile is presenting your Medininagar business correctly.

Confirming All Your Business Information Is Correct

Go to your GBP dashboard and review every single piece of information on your profile as it now appears publicly. Check the following:

  • Business name is spelled correctly and matches your official name
  • Address is accurate and the map pin is in the right location
  • Phone number is correct and working
  • Business category is the right one for your services
  • Business hours are accurate for every day of the week
  • Website URL (if added) goes to the correct page

If anything is incorrect, edit it immediately from your dashboard. It is much easier to fix errors now than after customers have already seen wrong information.

Making Sure Your Profile Is Visible on Google Maps

After verification, open a new browser tab and go to Google Maps. Search for your business name along with “Medininagar.” Your business should now appear as a pin on the map with your profile details showing on the left side.

Also do a Google Search for your business name. You should see your GBP knowledge panel appear on the right side of the search results – this is the box showing your name, address, phone number, hours, and map location.

If your profile does not appear immediately, do not worry. It can sometimes take 24 to 72 hours after verification for your profile to become fully visible across all of Google’s platforms. If it still does not appear after 72 hours, check your GBP dashboard for any pending actions or notifications.

Pro Tip: Search your business from a different device or in incognito mode to see exactly how it appears to customers. This gives you an accurate view without your own browsing history influencing the results.

Quick Tips Before You Hit Publish on Your GBP

Before you finalise your profile, run through these setup-specific tips to make sure you have not missed anything critical:

  • Use a local Medininagar phone number: Avoid toll-free or national numbers. A local phone number builds trust with Medininagar customers and signals to Google that you are genuinely a local business.
  • Double-check your spelling before submitting: Typos in your business name or address are surprisingly common and can cause verification delays and customer confusion. Read everything twice before clicking Submit.
  • Drop your map pin with precision: Zoom into the map as far as possible and place the pin on the exact building or entrance of your business – not just somewhere in the neighbourhood.
  • Do not use a virtual office or fake address: Google actively verifies business locations. Using a virtual office address or a residential address that does not match your actual operations can result in your profile being suspended.
  • Choose a storefront setup if you have any physical address available: Even for service-area businesses, a physical location GBP tends to rank better in competitive Medininagar searches than a service-area-only profile.
  • Keep your login details safe: Store your business Google account email and password securely. Losing access to your GBP account is one of the most frustrating problems a business owner can face.
