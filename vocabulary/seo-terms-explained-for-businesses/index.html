SEO Terms Explained for Businesses in Medininagar

If you are a business owner exploring SEO services in Medininagar, understanding common SEO terms can make digital marketing much easier. This simple glossary explains important concepts used in SEO marketing so you can better understand how search engines work and how businesses grow online.

Basic SEO Terms

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

SEO is the process of improving a website so it ranks higher on search engines like Google and attracts more organic visitors.

Keywords and Queries

Keywords are the words used to write content, while queries refer to the exact phrases users search to find information.

Search Engine Results Page (SERP)

SERP is the page displayed by search engines showing the results for a user’s search query.

On-Page SEO Terms

Title Tag

A title tag is the clickable headline that appears on search engine results and tells users what a webpage is about.

Meta Description

A meta description is a short summary of a webpage that appears under the title in search results.

Header Tags (H1, H2, H3)

Header tags organize content into sections and help both users and search engines understand the structure of a page.

Internal Linking

Internal linking means connecting one page of your website to another page within the same website.

Keyword Optimisation

Keyword optimisation is the practice of naturally placing relevant keywords within content so search engines understand the topic.

Content Optimisation

Content optimisation involves improving website content so it becomes more useful, relevant, and easier for search engines to rank.

Technical SEO Terms

Website Crawling

Crawling is the process where search engine bots scan webpages to discover new or updated content.

Website Indexing

Indexing is when search engines store webpages in their database so they can appear in search results.

XML Sitemap

An XML sitemap is a file that lists all important pages of a website to help search engines discover them.

Robots.txt

Robots.txt is a file that tells search engines which pages of a website they can or cannot crawl.

Page Speed

Page speed refers to how quickly a webpage loads for visitors.

Mobile-Friendly Website

A mobile-friendly website is designed to work properly on smartphones and tablets.

Local SEO Terms

Local SEO

Local SEO is the process of optimizing a business so it appears in location-based searches such as Search Engine Optimisation in Medininagar.

Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile is a free tool that helps businesses appear in Google Maps and local search results.

Local Citations

Local citations are online mentions of a business name, address, and phone number on directories or websites.

NAP Consistency

NAP consistency means maintaining the same Name, Address, and Phone number across all online listings.

Local Pack / Map Pack

The Local Pack is the map section that appears at the top of Google showing nearby businesses related to a search.

SEO Analytics & Performance Terms

Organic Traffic

Organic traffic refers to visitors who come to a website through unpaid search engine results.

Click Through Rate (CTR)

CTR is the percentage of users who click on a search result after seeing it.

Bounce Rate

Bounce rate is the percentage of visitors who leave a website without interacting with it.

Keyword Ranking

Keyword ranking indicates the position of a website on search engines for a specific keyword.

Conversion Rate

Conversion rate is the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action such as filling a form or making a purchase.
